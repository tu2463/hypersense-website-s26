With(in) is the culmination of the Seminar in Design Theory & Practice — taught by Dina El-Zanfaly & Marysol Ortega Pallanez for PhD and MDes students at Carnegie Mellon's School of Design.

We invite you to engage in a set of activities, conversations & games that reflect on the nature of a design process rooted in inquiry, introspection & sharing.

Five projects.

§ 02 — On view
i.

Proximity Garden

Proximity Garden is an interactive floor projection system that fosters connection between strangers through playful, embodied interaction. Using real‑time body tracking, the system detects interpersonal proximity and responds — lotus flowers blooming around people's feet.

ii.

It Takes Two to Make a Squiggle Wiggle

A tangible, AI‑assisted drawing experience that brings participants' sketches to life — supporting divergent thinking and social co‑creation in real time, while keeping the human as the dominant creative agent.

A human‑centered framing of AI‑assisted creativity, with implications for designing tangible co‑creative systems that preserve authorship and collaborative imagination.
