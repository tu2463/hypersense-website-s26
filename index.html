Proximity Garden

Proximity Garden is an interactive floor projection system that fosters connection between strangers through playful, embodied interaction. Using real-time body tracking, the system detects interpersonal proximity and responds by lotus flowers blooming around people's feet.

It Takes Two to Make a Squiggle Wiggle

This project investigates a tangible AI drawing experience that supports divergent thinking and social co-creation while keeping the human as the dominant creative agent. We contribute a human-centered framing of AI-assisted creativity and outlines implications for designing tangible co-creative systems that preserve authorship and collaborative imagination.

Zora

Zora is a smart scent diffuser that creates personalized olfactory environments through natural language voice or text input.

Users describe a desired feeling, vibe, or memory, and Zora interprets the prompt, factors in personal scent associations, and cross-references available cartridges to generate a custom blend while adjusting timing and intensity automatically. By learning about each individual's unique scent associations, it delivers experiences that feel personal and emotionally resonant. Zora reduces the cognitive load of scent selection and creates a guided experience for relaxation and wellbeing.

Peter Pan, Where Did the Shadow Go?

Peter Pan is a screenless generative AI interactive system that explores new possibilities for human‑AI interaction within physical spaces. Powered by spatial sensing and semantic reasoning, the AI interprets spatial configurations and human behaviors, then manifests itself through projected shadows in the environment.

Users engage in spatial negotiation with the agent by rearranging objects or through bodily intervention, creating a dynamic bi‑directional collaboration beyond one‑way command‑response patterns. Peter Pan investigates how context‑aware AI can establish a shared spatial agency grounded in the physical world.

The Space Between Us

The Space Between Us is a socially reactive installation that uses AI to track body and head orientation, rendering people's implicit non-verbal cues into a dynamic visual field. The installation turns shared space into a social canvas, and aims to explore what emerges between people when their fields meet.

Attunement: Transforming AI Latency into a "Social Alibi" for Non-Verbal Co-Creation

Although digital media eliminates geographical distance, it exacerbates people's social isolation in physical spaces. Although interactive art has explored how to reconstruct social connections, with the intervention of generative AI, how to naturally integrate the AI generation process into embodied interactions in physical space is still an underexplored area.

"Attunement" is an interactive prototype that combines real-time body rhythms with LLM music. Through the separation and convergence of visual colors, the installation guides strangers in public spaces to imitate and synchronize their bodies in a state of silence. The system transforms the network delay of AI-generated music DNA in the cloud into a "Kinetic Ritual" of "Incubating", turning the original technical limitations into a paving for emotional expectations.
